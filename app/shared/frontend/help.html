Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about using NexClaim.

Q1. What is NexClaim?

NexClaim is an AI-powered Insurance Claim Pre-Assessment Platform that helps process healthcare insurance claims using OCR, AI validation, risk assessment, and explainable recommendations.

Q2. How do I submit a claim?

Login to your Policyholder account, navigate to Submit Claim, upload the required documents, and click Process Claim.

Q3. What documents are required?

Mandatory documents:

  • Claim Form
  • Hospital Bill
  • Discharge Summary
  • Payment Receipt

Optional documents:

  • Prescription
  • Diagnostic Reports
  • Accident Report

Q4. How can I track my claim?

You can track claim status from your dashboard under My Claims.

Q5. What does Escalated mean?

Escalated means the claim requires additional review by an Admin due to risk factors, missing information, or validation concerns.

Q6. How secure is my information?

All documents and claim information are securely processed and access is restricted based on user roles.

Q7. Who makes the final decision?

AI provides recommendations, but final decisions are made by human reviewers and administrators.

Q8. What should I do if I forget my password?

Use the Forgot Password option on the login page to reset your password securely.
